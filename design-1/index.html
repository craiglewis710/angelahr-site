About

Angela Hughes

Principal HR Consultant, MCIPD, BA (Hons), ADR

I became an accredited member of the Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD) in 2000. I hold degrees in Business & Finance and Psychology, and I'm accredited with ADR as a workplace, commercial and civil mediator. I'm also a trained CBT counsellor, an experienced mentor and coach, and a board member of the Federation of International Employers (FedEE Global).

Corporate experience includes: ICAEW, Univar Europe, ArisGlobal Ltd, The Longhurst Group and Maclaren Ltd.

Sectors supported: engineering, food, manufacturing, distribution, housing associations, textiles, construction, retail, logistics, professional services, fast-growth start-ups, and union pay negotiations (Unite).
